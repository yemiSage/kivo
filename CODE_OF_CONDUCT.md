# Code of conduct

Kivo welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people's identities or worth.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels. Give actionable feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish personal information, credentials, private messages or customer data.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam, harassment, threats, sexualised conduct and discriminatory language.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact [the maintainer](https://github.com/yemiSage) through a private channel listed on their profile when available. Do not post sensitive allegations or personal data in a public issue. If no private channel is available, request one without disclosing sensitive details. For technical vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close discussions, restrict participation or ban participants according to the severity and repetition of the conduct. They should consider context, protect reporters' privacy as far as practical and avoid retaliation. Good-faith reports and respectful disagreement are welcome. Review is best-effort; this policy does not promise an emergency response service.
