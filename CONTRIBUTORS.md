## Contribution history

See the [GitHub contributors graph](https://github.com/yemiSage/kivo/graphs/contributors), merged pull requests and `git shortlog -s HEAD` for recorded contributions. Git author names may include historical aliases; a different author string alone does not establish another person.

Documentation, testing, design and accessibility contributions are welcome alongside code. Submit factual credit additions through a pull request with the contributor's consent. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to get started.

Kivo also depends on open-source projects and their communities. Their licenses and acknowledgements are separate from this contributor list; see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). AI assistance is tooling, not an invented human contributor.
