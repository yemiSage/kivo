Kivo

A little closer to everything worth keeping.

Click Kivo in your toolbar or right-click a link to save it. Kivo opens in a new tab and handles sign-in, saving and organisation.

Use the default Kivo address, your own HTTPS deployment, or localhost for development. Only use an app you trust: links you save are sent there.

No browsing-history collection. No page-content access. The extension stores only your Kivo address on this device. URLs and page titles are sent to that app only when you choose to save. Sign-in and saved data stay with your Kivo app and backend.
